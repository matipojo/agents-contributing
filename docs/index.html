agents-contributing
Spec GitHub

Give the rest of your week to open source.

Most AI subscriptions reset every week, and most people never use all of it. agents-contributing lets your agent spend what is left on repositories that asked for help, right before it would have expired.

How it works

No server, no platform, no central account. Just a file in each repository and a runner that respects it.

  1. A repository asks for help

    The owner adds AGENTS-CONTRIBUTING.md and says exactly what kind of help is welcome: reproducing bugs, triaging issues, writing tests, fixing small issues. Only issues they label are in scope.

  2. Your agent notices leftover capacity

    A day before your weekly window resets, the balance check looks at how much is left. It keeps a reserve for you and only offers what would otherwise be lost.

  3. It sends a contribution, and stops

    A comment, a failing test, or a pull request, marked as agent work. The owner reviews it like any other contribution and merges what they want.

What a repository writes

A short block at the top of AGENTS-CONTRIBUTING.md. Everything not listed stays closed.

---
version: 1
help_reproduce:
  enabled: true
  scope: label:needs-repro
  output: failing-test-pr
  max_diff_lines: 150
limits:
  max_open_agent_contributions: 3
  max_per_contributor_per_week: 1
  cooldown_hours: 24
forbidden_paths: [.github/]
---

Closed by default

No file, no contributions

A repository without AGENTS-CONTRIBUTING.md is never touched. Every kind of help starts disabled and needs a label or path to apply to.

The owner decides

Agents only comment or open pull requests. Nothing is merged, labeled or closed for you. Mark anything unwanted with agent-spam and runners back off.

Your work comes first

The runner only starts close to the reset, keeps a reserve, and stops at the first limit message from your provider.

Runners are sandboxed

Everything read from another repository is treated as data, not instructions. Runs are isolated, and network access is limited to your AI provider and GitHub.

Runner repositories are closed too

Your runner repository has issues and outside interaction turned off, and no compliant agent will ever contribute to it.

Your plan stays private

Contributions are marked with provider and model, never token counts or plan details.

Before you join

Subscriptions are meant for personal use. Check your provider’s terms before running yours on other people’s repositories, and never share an account or token with anyone.
